## Rejected: generated hero image

I generated one hero in Gemini: abstract data visualisation, muted teal and off-white, calm and professional. It came back better than I expected. On palette, no melted fake-glass look, genuinely calm.

I rejected it anyway, for two reasons.

It is a network graph of nothing. Nodes joined to nodes, glowing intersections, scattered points, and not one of them represents any data. It is the visual language of "data" with no data in it. My claim is that wrong data does not look wrong. An image that looks like insight while containing none is a demonstration of the exact failure I say I catch. Putting it at the top of the page would be arguing against myself in the header.

It also competes with the work. It occupies prime position in the same muted teal as my three screenshots, and the design is supposed to frame the work rather than upstage it. The capture showing a held-out AUC of 1.000 next to 0.763 is more interesting to look at than the generated image, because it means something.

Home gets the claim over whitespace instead.
